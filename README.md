## 🛠️ Web Development & Landing Pages
*Tools to build professional websites and landing pages with zero budget.*

*   **[Netlify](https://www.netlify.com)** - Perfect for deploying fast, static websites with free HTTPS, custom domains, and global CDN. *(Our portal is hosted here!)*
*   **[Vercel](https://vercel.com)** - Exceptional hosting platform for frontend frameworks (Next.js, React) with serverless functions and instant deploys.
*   **[Carrd](https://carrd.co)** - Simple, fully responsive, and modern one-page sites for pretty much anything.
*   **[Webflow](https://webflow.com)** - Build production-ready websites visually without writing code.
*   **[GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com)** - Host static sites directly from your GitHub repositories. Free and secure.

## ⚡ Automation & Workflows
*Integrate apps and automate repetitive tasks to save 20+ hours every week.*

*   **[n8n](https://n8n.io)** - Powerful node-based workflow automation tool that can be self-hosted completely free.
*   **[Make](https://www.make.com)** - Visual platform to design, build, and automate anything from simple tasks to complex workflows in minutes.
*   **[Zapier](https://zapier.com)** - Connect over 5,000+ apps to automate workflows easily. Very beginner-friendly.
*   **[Airtable](https://airtable.com)** - Part spreadsheet, part database. Perfect for structuring projects, content pipelines, and CRM systems.

## 🤖 AI Assistants & Productivity
*Supercharge your writing, brainstorming, and daily operations with artificial intelligence.*

*   **[Claude](https://claude.ai)** - Exceptional AI assistant developed by Anthropic. Highly recommended for complex coding, logical reasoning, and copywriting.
*   **[ChatGPT](https://chat.openai.com)** - The industry-standard AI chatbot for ideation, content creation, and technical support.
*   **[Notion](https://notion.so)** - The ultimate workspace. Organize your docs, projects, CRM, and content calendars in one unified place.
*   **[Perplexity AI](https://www.perplexity.ai)** - Conversational search engine that provides sourced answers in real-time. Excellent for market research.

## 🎨 Design, Assets & UI/UX
*Create stunning visuals and interactive user experiences without hiring a designer.*

*   **[Canva](https://www.canva.com)** - Drag-and-drop graphic design platform. Perfect for social media banners, ebooks, and slide decks.
*   **[Figma](https://figma.com)** - Collaborative web-based design tool. Excellent for prototyping landing pages and UI designs.
*   **[Unsplash](https://unsplash.com)** - Thousands of high-resolution, royalty-free stock photos editable for commercial use.
*   **[Excalidraw](https://excalidraw.com)** - Virtual whiteboard for sketching diagrams, database structures, and wireframes.

## 📈 Marketing, SEO & Newsletters
*Drive traffic, capture email leads, and build an audience of loyal customers.*

*   **[Beehiiv](https://www.beehiiv.com)** - The best newsletter platform for growth and monetization. Includes built-in referral systems and ad networks.
*   **[Substack](https://substack.com)** - Simple newsletter platform focusing on paid subscriptions and community building.
*   **[ConvertKit](https://convertkit.com)** - Professional email marketing platform for creators with powerful automation and landing page builders.
*   **[Google Analytics](https://analytics.google.com)** - Track your website traffic, user acquisition channels, and conversion metrics for free.

## 📦 Digital Products & Payments
*Collect payments globally and deliver downloadable assets automatically.*

*   **[Gumroad](https://gumroad.com)** - Sell digital products, memberships, and courses instantly with zero subscription fees (10% flat fee).
*   **[Payhip](https://payhip.com)** - Simple platform to sell ebooks, software, and templates. Very low transaction fees.
*   **[Stripe](https://stripe.com)** - The gold standard of online payment processing. Seamlessly integrates with almost every platform.
*   **[Lemon Squeezy](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com)** - Merchant of Record that handles global tax compliance and payouts for digital products and SaaS.
*   **[Digital Product Seller Ledger Sample](https://ronnie2025.github.io/xianyu-virtual-product-ledger-sample/)** - Free Excel/CSV ledger sample for tracking digital product orders, platform fees, costs, refunds, margins, and weekly review actions.
*   **[Side Hustle Monthly Report Generator](https://ronnie2025.github.io/side-hustle-monthly-report-generator/)** - Free browser-based monthly report generator for solopreneurs to turn orders, revenue, fees, refunds, and support time into a Markdown review and next-month action list.
